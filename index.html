PhoneCheck & Price

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

1. Introduction

PhoneCheck & Price (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is a phone hardware testing utility. It lets you run Auto and Manual tests, view Device Information, see a Phone Health Score, and generate a PDF report about your own device. This Privacy Policy explains what the App accesses, why, and how your information is handled.

In short: the App has no login, no registration, no database and no backend server. Everything runs on your device. We do not collect, store or sell your personal data.

2. Purpose of the App

The App helps each user check the condition of their own mobile phone: which components are working, which are not, and how the device has been holding up (for example battery, sensors, display, audio and connectivity). It is designed to test only the device it is installed on. It cannot be used to inspect, monitor or access anyone else’s device.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account, login, sign-up or registration is required or offered.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or any identity details.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, messages, call logs, photos or files.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, Firebase, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • We do not operate any server or database that stores user data.

4. Permissions We Request & Why

PermissionWhy it is needed
CameraOnly to test whether your front and rear cameras work. Permission is requested when you start the camera test, not at launch. No photos or video are saved or uploaded.
MicrophoneOnly for the speaker/microphone test, where a short tone is played and recorded to check the audio level. Audio is analysed on the device and is not saved or uploaded.
VibrationOnly to test the vibration motor. This permission does not access any personal data.

Permissions are used only while the related test is running.

5. Device Information Accessed

To show you the state of your phone, the App reads technical details such as brand, model, chipset, RAM, storage, display, build details, battery level and temperature, and sensor availability. This information is read on your device only, is shown to you inside the App, and is never uploaded or shared with us or anyone else.

6. Network Usage

The App makes a single optional network request: the internet speed test downloads a test file from a public endpoint (speed.cloudflare.com) to measure your download speed. No personal data, device identifiers or test results are sent in this process. Standard connection data (such as your IP address) is naturally visible to that endpoint, as with any internet request, and is governed by Cloudflare’s own privacy policy.

7. Data Storage

The App stores only a simple onboarding flag (whether you have seen the introduction screens) on your device using local storage (shared_preferences). Nothing else is saved. Uninstalling the App removes this data.

8. PDF Reports & Sharing

PDF reports are generated on your device. We never receive them. Sharing is entirely your choice: the only data that leaves your phone is a report that you yourself decide to share, print or save, using the options provided by your device. The App does not send reports anywhere automatically.

9. Third-Party Services

The App does not include any third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or tracking. The only external service involved is the Cloudflare public speed test endpoint described in Section 6.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected, there is nothing to disclose or delete. The App is not directed at children, but it is safe to use as it contains no accounts, ads or tracking.

11. Data Security

Because your data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us, the risk of it being exposed through our systems is minimal. Reports you share are protected only by the method you choose to share them, so please share them with people you trust.

12. Your Rights & Control

  • You can revoke Camera or Microphone permission at any time in your phone’s Settings > Apps > PhoneCheck & Price > Permissions.
  • You can use the App without granting optional permissions; only the related test will be unavailable.
  • You can remove all App data at any time by clearing its storage or uninstalling it.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
